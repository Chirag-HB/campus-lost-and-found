⚡ Simple Process

How CampusFinder Works

Getting your belongings back (or helping someone else) takes just a few steps.

01
📝

Report Your Item

Fill out a quick form with details about your lost or found item — description, location, date, and a photo.

→
02
🔔

Get Notified

Our system matches your item with other listings. You'll get an instant notification when there's a match.

→
03
🤝

Connect & Claim

Chat securely through the portal, verify your identity, and pick up your item from the campus security office.

→
04
🎉

Reunited!

Mark the item as returned and help us keep track of our campus success rate. Your item is home!
